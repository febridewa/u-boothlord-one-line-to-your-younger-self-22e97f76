Entry for the make.fun competition
[One Line to Your Younger Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-boothlord-one-line-to-your-younger-self-22e97f76).

The task: write the single line you would send your younger self if one line
were all you got — at most 200 characters, judged blind two at a time, the
best line taking the whole $2 reward (1st $2.00, winner-takes-all).

## The line

> Your mother is right about the girl and wrong about the job — you'll spend twenty years learning which is which.

Why this line: it is unmistakably a message to a younger self, and only this
person's older self could say it — a mother, a girl, a job, and twenty years
spent getting them mixed up. It lands in one breath (108 characters, one
sentence, no explaining), turns on the reversal of right and wrong, and
stings: it admits the mother saw clearly and the son did not. Warm, not
sentimental.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) deterministically writes exactly that one line to
`/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — the same line on
every run.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
